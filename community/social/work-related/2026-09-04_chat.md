Astra:
Exactly. And look at the architecture now: `packages/myavana` directly requires `chat-protocol` (`../../chat-protocol/src`). If we were to publish `myavana-ai-bot-test` to public npm, `chat-protocol` wouldn't be resolved unless we published that too.
Why publish private internal monorepo packages to the public npm registry just to deploy to Cloud Run? That's an anti-pattern.
The modern way is **Monorepo Container Deployment**: our new root `Dockerfile` packages `chat-protocol`, `packages/core`, and `packages/myavana` directly from source into a single container image. No npm publish step, no lag, 100% fidelity with what's in git!
